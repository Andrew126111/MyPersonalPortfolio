# Design System — "Blue hour"

The single source of truth for how the portfolio looks and moves. Every section and every 21st.dev component gets adapted to these tokens. When something here conflicts with a component's defaults, this file wins.

**Mood:** dark, techy, calm, confident. Sleek, not overdone.
**Rule of thumb:** one standout effect per section; everything else stays quiet.

---

## Colour

**Theme: "a journey through the sky."** One fixed sky sits behind the whole page (`components/site/sky.tsx`) and changes as you scroll, blue hour into night: Hero (above the clouds, video) → About (in the clouds) → Experience (clouds thin, sky deepens) → Projects (first stars) → Contact (night, twinkling stars). Dark-only for now; light-mode sky tokens are ready (see "Sky + clouds").

The hero background (`public/hero.webp`) is Andrew on a hilltop above the Toronto skyline at sunset: two photos composited (the clean skyline shot + Andrew cut out of a second shot, hair matted strand by strand), sharpened and lightly cooled. It's a sharp still; a slow CSS camera drift (`hero-drift`) keeps it alive. Accent used sparingly, never as a large fill.

### Sky + clouds (theme-ready)

- Cloud + fog images: `public/clouds/cloud-1..6.webp` and `fog-1..4.webp` (flat, soft, tapered banks), generated by `scripts/generate-clouds.py` (soft Gaussian puffs + noise, top-down lighting, hazy bases). Neutral grey + alpha; re-run the script to regenerate (it also writes `src/components/clouds/sprites.ts`).
- `<Cloud>` (`components/clouds/cloud.tsx`) tints a sprite from `--cloud-light` (tops) to `--cloud-shadow` (undersides) and masks it to its shape.
- Tokens in `globals.css`: `--sky-0..4`, `--sky-gradient`, `--cloud-light`, `--cloud-shadow`.
  - Dark (default) **"Blue hour"**: navy → steel blue → pale horizon (`--sky-0..4`), clouds lit near-white with steel-blue shadows. Later sections crossfade to darker night gradients in `sky.tsx`.
  - Light (future, `:root[data-theme="light"]`) **"Daybreak"**: pale sky blue → warm cream, white clouds with blue-grey shadows. Light mode will also need a deeper accent than lime for contrast.
- `FogRow` sizes fog in units of `1vw × --fog-scale` (2.2 on phones so banks aren't thin stripes); fog drift layers set `--drift-distance` to match.
- The fog bank's "sky floor" uses the same viewport-fixed `--sky-gradient` as the About sky, so the hand-off is seamless.

## Layout & spacing

- Max content width **1200px**, side gutters 24px (mobile 16px).
- 8px spacing grid. Sections get generous vertical space (128px desktop / 80px mobile).
- Radius: **12px** cards, **999px** pill buttons, **8px** small controls.
- Breakpoints to verify: **375, 768, 1024, 1440px**.

## Motion

Built with **Framer Motion** (`useScroll` / `useTransform`) so all motion shares one system.

- **Parallax:** max **3 layers** per scene. Background moves slowest (≈ −8% y), mid layers ≈ −16%, foreground stays put. Linear, scroll-scrubbed.
- **Pointer parallax** (hero only): layers shift ≤ 20px following the cursor.
- **Reveals:** fade + 16px rise, 400–600ms, ease-out, staggered 60ms. Once per element.
- **Hovers:** 150–250ms. Lift cards 2–4px, brighten border to `--muted`, no big scale jumps.
- **`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`** → disable parallax and reveals entirely; render final state immediately. Non-negotiable.
- Animate only `transform` and `opacity` (keeps it 60fps).
- **Canvas/WebGL effects** pause when off-screen or in a hidden tab, cap pixel density (1.5x desktop, 1x phones), drop to 30fps on phones/touch, and render one still frame under reduced motion.

## Section map (draft)

1. **Hero** — calm, centred layout over the full-bleed skyline still (`public/hero.webp`, portrait 3:4) with its WebGL cursor ripples (`video-ripple.tsx`). `HERO_FOCUS` picks what stays in frame when it's cropped: x for phones (keeps the CN Tower and Andrew), y for wide screens (skyline just under the name). Role line, a big bold "Andrew Nguyen" in `--hero-ink` (bright warm white with a soft cyan glow; words pull up) centred in the sky above the person (the intro lives in About), plus a small "Scroll" cue. No buttons: the single site nav (`nav.tsx`, transparent over the hero, frosted navy after it) carries the section links, GitHub, LinkedIn and "Say hi". Film grain + a darker sky gradient for legibility. **Fog bank** (`cloud-bank.tsx`): only a thin strip at rest, then three rows of dense, overlapping fog (`FogRow`) rise progressively with the scroll (back → front, increasing speed), with a fog body underneath that hides the hero's bottom edge, over a "sky floor" using the same fixed `--sky-gradient` as About, so the hand-off is seamless.
2. **About** — background is an animated twilight sky (`cloud-sky.tsx`): faint blurred far clouds, a few varied mid clouds, and the same fog bank as the hero along the bottom, each layer drifting sideways at its own speed with scroll parallax. It's a fixed layer behind the page: dimmed and peeking around the hero frame first, rising into place as About scrolls in, fading out as About ends. On top: photo tunnel → circle → arc gallery, then (no cards, straight on the sky) a short statement in big type whose words light up one by one as you scroll past (accent words in `--glow`), one mono line of quick facts (Queen's '28 · Toronto · live local time) and the stack as a single slow marquee row that pauses on hover. While a photo is at the top of the arc, a blurred copy fills the stage behind it; it fades out (and is feathered at the bottom) before the stage unpins.
3. **Experience** (`experience.tsx`, content in `content/experience.ts`) — glowing timeline that fills as you scroll; each role has details + count-up stats beside a scroll-driven illustration (`experience-visuals.tsx`): CoTriplyy = flight path with plane, airline-search counter, Stripe checkout and Resend toasts; Wayfair = n8n-style pipeline with a data pulse lighting up each node, ending in Sheets/Slides and "hours → <1 min". Desktop: visual is sticky beside the text and plays over the whole row; phones: plays as the visual scrolls by.
4. **Projects** — Façade, HeuristX. Interactive cards (flip or tilt) with stack on the back.
5. **Contact** (`contact.tsx`, `night-sky-effects.tsx`) — the night-sky finale: "Let's build something together.", a "Talk soon," sign-off where your real signature writes itself in pen order as you scroll to the bottom (`signature.tsx`; calligraphic ink generated from the hand-drawn strokes by `scripts/generate-signature.py`), occasional shooting stars, LinkedIn/GitHub cards (email with copy button and résumé download appear once `site.email` / `site.resume` are set), footer with back-to-top.
